## Where Q29's theme comes from

Q28 asked which way each check fails, and its units landed: every census member carries a direction,
the flattering numbers are classified, the unfalsifiable excuses are enumerated, the recorded-and-
never-applied remedies are applied, and W363 re-read the quarter's own gate and found every check
the horizon named answered. What Q29 comes from is not whether Q28 met its gate but what the
quarter kept finding on the way — and it kept finding the same thing, in nine units by two builders.

**The comparison was right and the population was wrong.** Over and over, the defect was not in what
a check decided but in *who was in the room when it decided*.

**W353 named it and it kept happening after.** A derivation handed something it does not understand
that WIDENS instead of throwing: `quarterModules` in the pre-W342 shape returned 295 modules where
the quarter had eleven. W362's mutation run over Q27 found exactly one survivor in sixty-four, and
it was the same shape one layer in — flipping `s.kind === "unit"` to its inverse in
`unitFieldTypings` gives a WIDER field set that shares `by`, `id` and `value` with the right one, so
`looseTwins` still found nothing and the strict-typing floor still cleared. Every assertion over the
wrong population passed.

**Three units in a row built the wrong population first and were caught by driving it.** W359's
store dependence walked *through* store modules and reported thirty gaps where eight were real,
because `complaints/store.ts` imports `booking/store.ts`. W361's zero census took every counting
expression in every interpolation and got fifty-nine sites where the console renders twenty-seven,
putting an import list and a destructuring on the "screen". W355's drive census counted each
function's own DECLARATION as a call to itself, which made every defaulted parameter in the tree
look driven by the module that declares it. None of those was a wrong comparison. Each was a
correct comparison over a population nobody had checked.

**And the narrow direction is worse, because it is silent.** W360's pass found `private-copies.ts`
missing the eighth copy of the ledger parse — `timelines.ts` spells it `startsWith("| ")` and the
register's marker is the regex `/^\|`, so a copy written a different way is not a copy at all. It
found `assertion-vocabulary.ts` parsing an excuse key of `<file> :: <site>` and keeping only the
file, so one exemption silences a whole file's presence claims. W358 found nine e2e specs walking a
premise nothing had checked; W359 found three more resetting stores they did not read. W362 had to
add `UNMUTATED_AT_W362`, because a module the operators find nothing to change in and a module the
sweep cleared read identically in a survivor count.

**The tree has no register of populations.** It has W267's census of registers, W352's directions,
W353's superset probes and W355's defaults — each about one property of a check. What none of them
holds is the population itself: what a check is over, how that set is derived, and what would be in
it that is not. A green run says the comparison agreed with the declaration. It says nothing about
who was missing.

So: **Q29 — what the check is over.** Every unit takes a check in this tree and establishes its
population: derived rather than listed, resolved against the tree, and shown reporting something
that belongs and refusing something that does not. Where the population is narrower than the
check's own claim, the unit says so or widens it. The gate follows Q25's, Q26's, Q27's and Q28's
shape rather than a number, because Q24's was a number and the number measured the wrong thing: the
gate is that **every population this document names is derived, and is shown both including a
planted member and excluding a planted non-member**, and W376 re-reads the list.

Nothing here needs a ruling; nothing adds a blocked row.

## What this expansion does not do

- **It does not write Q30.** One quarter at a time. W377 expands the next when it arrives, from
  what Q29 finds rather than from what this document guesses.
- **It does not set a numeric gate.** Q24's was a number, the number moved the wrong way, and that
  is recorded in `docs/HORIZON-Q25.md` where the correction was made. Four quarters have now
  refused a number and each gate worked; repeating what works is not novelty for its own sake.

- **It does not propose an eleventh gate.** Q29 crosses none.

  for three hundred and forty-six units, and building faster does not shorten that. What the loop
  can do is make sure the wait is visible and correctly counted, which is what W347 and W361 did to
  the founder's own page.
